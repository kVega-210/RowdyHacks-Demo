HEIST HAVOC!

Rob the bank. Sabotage your friends. Bank the most. 2-8 players, about 10 minutes.

Join

  1. Scan the QR on the big screen (or open the link and type the 4-letter code).
  2. Pick a name. You get a random animal face: rivals target you by it.

Each round

  1. Briefing: your phone secretly shows your target.
  2. Play: crack minigames as fast as you can. Win = cash into your wallet. Fail = small fine.
  3. Results: out-earn your target for a bounty.
  4. Break: pick a sabotage.

Money

  • Wallet: what you've earned. Fines come out of it and go back into the bank.
  • Bank: paid out over the host's chosen rounds. Cash left after the last round? Overtime until it's empty.
  • The end: when the bank hits $0, every wallet is banked automatically. Biggest stash wins.

Events: react fast!

FREEZE!A siren rises first. Then hands off your phone: the clock stops for everyone, touching costs you.

Dirty tricks

From round 2, pick a rival's face and a trick: shrink their buttons, shake their screen, fake an alarm, jam the signal. Each trick hits one job, never two jobs in a row, and nobody can be picked two breaks running.

House rules: no peeking at other phones during a FREEZE, and the host's word on FREEZE is final.
