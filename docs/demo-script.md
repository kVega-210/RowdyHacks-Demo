> Drafted; rehearse twice with a timer (human).

**0:00 Hook (20s).** "Party games make you stare at your phone. HEIST HAVOC! makes you grab your friend's keys."
Hold up a key on a lanyard.

**0:20 Live game (110s).** Judges scan the QR (pre-made room, 4 players; fill the rest with bots). Start.
Point at the speed ramp, let one STEAL happen live with a real key (or the virtual picker), call FREEZE.
Show a sabotage hitting a phone (Mirror Mode). Hit ESCAPE.

**2:10 Sponsors (35s).** The narrator voice is ElevenLabs. The roast is Gemini. Every steal and fail streams into a
Tiger Data hypertable: open `/replay/` for the cash-over-time chart. It runs on a Vultr VM at our GoDaddy domain.

**2:45 Close (15s).** "36 minigames, 7 sabotages, one shrinking bank. Escape rich."

Fallbacks: no Wi-Fi => laptop hotspot, host "Fill with bots"; no audio => Web Speech narrator; no AI keys =>
template roast. Backup video per DEMO-03.
