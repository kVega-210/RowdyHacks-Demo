HEIST HAVOC!

Rob the bank. Rob your friends. Escape rich. 2-8 players, about 10 minutes.

Join

  1. Scan the QR on the big screen (or open the link and type the 4-letter code).
  2. Pick a name. Grab a key and scan its tag to claim your vault (if keys are on).

Each round

  1. Briefing: your phone secretly shows your target.
  2. Play: crack minigames as fast as you can. Win = cash into your wallet. Fail = small fine.
  3. Results: out-earn your target for a bounty.
  4. Break: draw a card, pick a sabotage.

Money

  • Wallet: at risk. Can be stolen. Lost if you don't escape.
  • Stash: safe. Only the Escape fills it. Biggest stash wins.
  • Bank: shrinks all game. At $0, everyone must escape.

Events: react fast!

STEAL!Grab a rival's key, scan it. First valid scan wins a cut of their wallet. FREEZE!Hands off your phone. Touching it costs you. BANK RAID!Smash GRAB. First 3 split a bonus. ESCAPE!Tap ESCAPE to bank your wallet before the doors lock.

Cards

In a break, draw a card and enter its number. Do the dare; the crew votes PASS or FAIL. Physical cards can always be skipped for free, no questions asked.

Dirty tricks

From round 2, pick a sabotage for a rival: mirror their screen, shrink their buttons, fake an alarm...

House rules: be nice to the keys, no grabbing keys out of hands (the table is fair game), and the host's word on FREEZE is final.
